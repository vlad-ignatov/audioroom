## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-26

The first public release.

### Added

- **Internet radio:** search about 50,000 stations from radio-browser.info,
  HLS included, and keep them in a sortable, filterable library with
  favorites and saved views. The current artist and title show in the
  window title and the OS media controls, where the station sends them.
- **Local music:** browse folders and single files as a tree with tags and
  cover art, and play them from a queue you can fill by dragging. ALAC,
  AIFF, CAF and WMA files are converted in the background.
- **Tag editing** for MP3 files, one at a time or a folder at once, and a
  repair tool for Cyrillic tags saved in the wrong encoding.
- **Sound:** a 10-band equalizer with presets, a multiband limiter that
  keeps boosts from clipping, loudness leveling across stations and tracks,
  reverb with five rooms, and stereo width.
- **Desktop:** native menus and right-click menus, media keys, keyboard
  shortcuts, and backup and restore of the library and sound setup.
- Packages for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux.
